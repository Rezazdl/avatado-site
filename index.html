How it works

Live in three steps

  1. 1

    Sit in front of your webcam

    Any ordinary webcam works. No phone, no suit, no markers. Look at the screen and press Calibrate once.

  2. 2

    Pick your character

    Start with a free avatar, buy one from the shop, or order a character made just for you.

  3. 3

    Choose “Avatado Camera”

    Select it in OBS, Zoom, Discord or Teams like any other webcam. In OBS you can also use Spout2 with a transparent background.

Help

Questions people ask

Which apps can I use it with?

Any app that lets you choose a camera: OBS Studio, Zoom, Google Meet, Discord, Microsoft Teams and streaming tools. Select “Avatado Camera” as your webcam. In OBS you can also add it through Spout2 with a transparent background.

Do I need an iPhone or a special camera?

No. A normal webcam is enough. Avatado reads your face, body, hands and fingers from that one camera.

Does my video go online?

No. Tracking runs on your PC, and the camera image is never uploaded. Recordings are saved only on your computer, and only when you press Record.

Is it free?

The app is free during early access. Avatars from the shop and custom characters are paid.

Can I use my own 3D model?

Not yet. Avatars are made by the Avatado studio so they track well from the start. If you want your own character, request a custom one.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Yes, this happens with new apps. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The installer only installs Avatado and the Avatado Camera.
